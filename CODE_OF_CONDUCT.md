# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1 (licensed CC BY 4.0). This is a short adaptation for a small project.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Give and accept feedback graciously; focus on the work, not the person.
- Use welcoming, inclusive language. Issues and pull requests may be written in English or Spanish.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Trolling, or repeated disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that do not follow them, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they consider inappropriate.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email listed on their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.
